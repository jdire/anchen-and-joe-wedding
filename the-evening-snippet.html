🌙 Evening Celebrations

◆

7:30pm — Cake Cutting & First Dance

We'll be cutting the cake and taking to the dance floor for our first dance around 7:30pm.

Evening guests aren't expected to arrive for this — but if you happen to be turning up around this time, feel free to quietly filter in and join the atmosphere as things get started.

7:45pm — Live Music & Disco

From 7:45pm, the incredible The Takeover will be bringing the energy with live music and a full evening disco. 

8:45pm — Evening food Served

At 8:45pm, evening food arrives in the form of Dirty Dogs from Duck & Strawberry — a staffed buffet featuring:

  • Traditional sausages
  • Soft white finger rolls
  • Selection of toppings: crispy onions, pulled pork, crispy bacon, cheese, mustard, ketchup

11:30pm — Wind-Down

We kindly ask guests to start making their way out from 11:30pm so we can wrap up smoothly.

12:00am — Hard Close

The venue has a strict midnight finish, so we'll need everyone to have departed by 12:00am.

Thank you for helping us keep things running smoothly — and for celebrating with us right to the end.
